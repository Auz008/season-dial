SEASON DIAL — A LITTLE WORLD, FOR YOU

MacBook Air version (13.6-inch screen assumption)
- desktop-*.png: four native-size 2560 x 1664 wallpaper images.
- season-dial-macbook-air.mp4: 40-second, 1920 x 1248, 24 fps film.

11-inch iPad Pro version (M4/M5 screen format assumption)
- landscape-*.png: four 2420 x 1668 wallpaper images.
- portrait-*.png: four 1668 x 2420 wallpaper images.
- season-dial-ipad.mp4: 40-second, 1810 x 1248, 24 fps landscape film.
- season-dial-ipad-portrait.mp4: 40-second, 1248 x 1812, 24 fps portrait film.
All films are H.264 MP4. Images use the device's native size; films use its
proportions at a practical HD resolution. Earlier iPad Pro models have a
slightly different width, so crop or fit the image in Wallpaper settings.

Living scene
https://auz008.github.io/season-dial/?wallpaper=1
Use Safari Share > Add to Home Screen on iPad for a clean standalone view.
Double-tap the scene to show or hide controls. The artwork has no printed
name, title or message, so it can be given as a quiet visual gift.

Spring: tree blossoms and a small colorful wildflower garden.
Summer: lush green foliage and delicate white dandelion seed heads.
Autumn: warm leaves and red apples.
Winter: a snow-tipped Christmas tree, tiny warm lights and fine snow grains.
The cats have a soft glow and take occasional, staggered little jumps.

Wallpaper use
Save a PNG and choose it in the system's Wallpaper settings.
The MP4 files are films, not installable system-wallpaper packages or Live
Photos. Opening the web scene gives continuous animation while it is open.
iPad Lock Screen motion uses a compatible Live Photo, depends on the device
and iPadOS, and plays on wake; an MP4 cannot be installed directly as an
endlessly animated iPad system wallpaper. No wallpaper app is installed.
